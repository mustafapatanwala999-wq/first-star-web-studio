Privacy

Last updated 10 November 2026

First Star Web Studio designs websites for businesses. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it. In short: very little, and we never sell it.

This website

This site uses no analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

When you contact us

If you email us or send the request form, we receive your name, email address, website and message. The form is delivered to our inbox by the service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We use these details only to reply to you and to prepare the design you asked for. We do not sell or share them.

Our social media tool

We run a small internal tool that posts our own videos to First Star's own Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts. It connects only to those accounts. It does not access, collect or store data about any other person. Its connection keys are kept privately on our office computer and are never shared.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete anything we hold about you, email firststarwebstudio@gmail.com. We will act on it promptly.
